What does my torque wrench setting do?

Torque chart for this class and thread condition

T = K × d × F. Clamp load F is a share of the bolt's proof load (default 75%). Strengths and stress areas from ISO 898-1 (class 8.8 proof stress is 580 MPa up to M16, 600 MPa above). K (nut factor) is an estimate: 0.20 dry, 0.15 lubricated, 0.13 zinc flake coating. Real K varies a lot with finish, washers and thread condition, so the same torque can give 25% more or less clamp load. Torque only indirectly measures tension. Torque numbers need a nut or tapped hole as strong as the bolt. For critical or safety joints (wheels, brakes, pressure parts, structure) use the manufacturer's torque spec. Source: Fastenal torque-tension table, ISO 898-1
